# Provenance

Use when origin, transformation, or verification of content matters (Generation AI kit §14). Do not reduce this to a decorative HUMAN / AI badge.

## Axes

| Axis | Values |
|---|---|
| Origin | `human` · `AI-generated` · `mixed` · `unknown` |
| Transformation | `edited` · `translated` · `summarized` · `enhanced` |
| Verification | `verified` · `unverified` · `disputed` |
| History | `original` · `updated` · `corrected` |

## Disclosure

| Risk | Surface |
|---|---|
| Low | On demand |
| Medium | Compact visible label |
| High | Label plus source or review summary |
| Consequential | Full review before reliance |

Illustrative or synthetic media that could be mistaken for fact must be labelled. This pairs with Dutch data-honesty (`SAMPLE DATA`).
